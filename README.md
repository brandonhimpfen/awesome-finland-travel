## Official & Planning

Essential official tourist boards, entry guidelines, and weather services for Finland.

- [Visit Finland](https://www.visitfinland.com/en/) - Official national travel guide featuring seasonal highlights, itinerary routes, and regional guides.
- [Police of Finland (Poliisi)](https://poliisi.fi/en/frontpage) - Official Finnish police portal covering passport requirements, permits, and legal regulations.
- [FMI Weather (Finnish Meteorological Institute)](https://en.ilmatieteenlaitos.fi/) - Official national weather service providing accurate forecasts, aurora borealis warnings, and climate tracking.

## Transportation

Navigating national railways, regional bus networks, ferry systems, and air routes.

- [VR (Finnish Railways)](https://www.vr.fi/en) - National passenger train operator connecting Helsinki with Lapland, Lakeland, and coastal hubs.
- [Matkahuolto](https://www.matkahuolto.fi/en) - Comprehensive national long-distance bus and coach route planner across Finland.
- [HSL Helsinki Region Transport](https://www.hsl.fi/en) - Official public transit authority for metro, tram, bus, and commuter ferry lines in the capital area.
- [Finnair](https://www.finnair.com/en) - National flag carrier operating international gateways and domestic connections to Lapland (Rovaniemi, Ivalo, Kittilä).

## Destinations & Regions

### Helsinki & Capital Region

- [My Helsinki](https://www.myhelsinki.fi/) - Official local-backed city guide detailing design districts, museum passes, and public saunas.
- [Visit Porvoo](https://www.porvoo.fi/en/tourism-and-leisure/) - Official guide to Finland's second-oldest town, featuring historic wooden merchant houses and riverside vistas.

### Lakeland

- [Visit Tampere](https://visittampere.fi/en/) - Visitor portal for the Sauna Capital of the World, ridge trails, and lakeside cafes.
- [Visit Jyväskylä Region](https://visitjyvaskyla.fi/en/) - Regional guide highlighting Alvar Aalto architecture, lakes, and cultural events.

### Coast & Archipelago

- [Visit Turku](https://en.visitturku.fi/) - Official guide for Finland's historic former capital, River Aura culture, and archipelago ferry rings.

### Lapland & Arctic

- [House of Lapland](https://www.lapland.fi/) - Official regional portal for Arctic wilderness, Sámi cultural heritage, and midnight sun or polar night experiences.
- [Rovaniemi Tourist Information](https://www.visitroviemi.fi/) - Official visitor hub for the Arctic Circle, Santa Claus Village, and winter husky excursions.

## National Parks & Nature

Resources for exploring Finland's 41 national parks, wilderness areas, and outdoor access rules.

- [Nationalparks.fi (Metsähallitus)](https://www.nationalparks.fi/) - Official portal for all 41 Finnish national parks, hiking maps, trail conditions, and wilderness hut bookings.
- [Everyman's Right (Jokamiehenoikeus)](https://www.nationalparks.fi/everymansright) - Official guide to Finland's legal framework allowing free roaming, foraging, and camping in nature.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers, remote workers, and startup professionals in Finland.

- [Work in Finland (Business Finland)](https://www.workinfinland.com/) - Official government hub providing relocation guides, startup permits, and remote professional resources.
- [Maria 01 Startup Hub](https://maria.io/) - Leading Nordic startup campus and community network based in Helsinki.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical guidance on emergency contacts, winter driving, and outdoor survival.

- [Emergency Number 112 Finland](https://112.fi/en) - Direct nationwide emergency contact number for medical, police, and fire assistance.
- [112 Suomi App](https://112.fi/en/112-suomi-application) - Official emergency mobile app that automatically transmits exact GPS coordinates during distress calls.
